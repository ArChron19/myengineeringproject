The Engineering Approach to Test-Taking

Speed + Precision = Success

Both engineering work and exam success share a common goal: reaching the answer efficiently. Here's a professional secret that can transform your test-taking strategy:

You Don't Need Exact Solutions

  • Professional engineers typically work within 3 significant digits

  • For multiple-choice exams, 1-2 significant digits is often sufficient to identify the correct answer

  • Getting to a reliable estimate quickly beats calculating exact values slowly

  • selectNsolve™ embraces this practical engineering mindset, teaching students to:

  • Solve problems strategically rather than mechanically

  • Build engineering intuition alongside mathematical skills

  • Focus on understanding concepts, not just crunching numbers
