Privacy Policy

Making sure your privacy and data is always safe.

  • My Engineering Project LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is the developer of selectNsolve™ Basic, a Google Sheets add-on. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users' personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our add-on (the "Add-On").

    By installing and using the Add-On, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not install or use the Add-On.

  • This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the selectNsolve™ Basic Google Sheets add-on and any related services provided by My Engineering Project LLC. It does not apply to third-party websites, services, or applications that may be accessed through links within the Add-On.

  • 3.1 Google Account Information

    When you install and authorize the Add-On, we collect your Google account email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) as provided by Google during the OAuth authorization process.

    3.2 Authorization Scopes

    We collect information about the specific permissions you grant to the Add-On during the OAuth consent flow, which allow us to access and interact with your Google Sheets data as necessary for the Add-On's functionality.

    3.3 Usage Data

    We collect anonymous usage statistics, including:

    • Feature usage frequency and patterns

    • Error logs and crash reports

    • Performance metrics

    • Technical data such as browser type, operating system version, and device information

    3.4 Information We Do Not Collect

    We do not collect, store, or transmit the contents of your Google Sheets documents to our servers unless explicitly required for a specific feature's functionality, and only for the duration necessary to perform the requested operation.

  • We use collected information for the following purposes:

    • Authentication: To verify your identity and authorize access to Google Sheets through Google's OAuth 2.0 protocol

    • Add-On Functionality: To provide, maintain, and improve the features and services of the Add-On

    • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and optimize performance

    • Customer Support: To respond to inquiries, provide technical assistance, and troubleshoot problems

    • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

  • 5.1 Security Measures

    We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

    • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL protocols

    • Secure storage with encryption at rest where applicable

    • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only

    • Regular security assessments and updates

    5.2 Data Retention

    We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law. Anonymous usage statistics may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

    5.3 Data Location

    Your data may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers maintain facilities.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

    • Google LLC: To facilitate authentication and authorization through Google's services and to enable Add-On functionality within Google Sheets

    • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who provide infrastructure, hosting, maintenance, and support services on our behalf, subject to confidentiality obligations

    • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, governmental regulation, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others

    • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, where your information may be transferred to the successor entity

  • Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve the Add-On's functionality and do not transfer this data to third parties except as disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

  • You have the following rights regarding your information:

    • Revoke Authorization: You may revoke the Add-On's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

    • Access and Correction: You may request access to or correction of your personal information by contacting us at help@myengineeringproject.com

    • Data Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain data

    • Uninstall: You may uninstall the Add-On at any time through Google Sheets or the Google Workspace Marketplace

  • The Add-On is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

  • If you are accessing the Add-On from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. By using the Add-On, you consent to such transfer and processing.

  • We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting. We will notify users of material changes through the Add-On, our website, or via email. Your continued use of the Add-On after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

  • If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

    My Engineering Project LLC
    Email: help@myengineeringproject.com

  • This Privacy Policy is effective as of 11/4/2025.
