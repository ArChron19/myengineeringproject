Questions About Donations?

Is my donation tax-deductible?
My Engineering Project LLC is a business, not a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations are not tax-deductible. However, your contribution directly funds educational access.

How is my donation used?
Every dollar goes toward server costs, development, support, and keeping selectNsolve Basic™ free for all users.

Can I donate on behalf of my school or organization?
Absolutely! Contact us at help@myengineeringproject.com for acknowledgment options or bulk support arrangements.

Will selectNsolve Basic™ always be free?
Core equation-solving features will always remain free. Future premium features (like advanced multi-variable solving) may be offered as optional add-ons, but the tool you know today stays accessible to everyone.
