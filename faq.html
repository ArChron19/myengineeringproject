Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

  • What is the primary function of the selectNsolve Basic™ Add-on?

    selectNsolve™ Basic is a Google Sheets™ Add-on designed to improve test-taking efficiency and engineering problem-solving. It processes simple text input and arrangements, allowing users to quickly solve and model equations without needing an in-depth understanding of complex spreadsheet functions.

  • What platform is required to use the selectNsolve™ Basic tool?

    The selectNsolve™ Basic Add-on is integrated with Google Sheets™. This allows users to leverage a free, versatile, and collaborative platform for mathematical demonstrations and real-time joint modeling sessions.

  • How does this approach transform traditional homework?

    While AI has transformed homework into a question-and-answer session, our approach focuses on empowering students and teachers to use tools like Google Sheets to interact with mathematical demonstrations and perform rapid, real-time modeling, moving beyond basic tabular data organization.

  • Why does My Engineering Project LLC state that finding an exact solution is often unnecessary?

    In both professional engineering and standardized test-taking, finding the exact solution is often less critical than speed and approximation. Most engineers are content with answers accurate to three significant digits, and knowing an exam problem's value within one to two significant digits is typically sufficient to choose the correct answer from multiple-choice options.

  • What is the focus of the Practical Engineering section of the website?

    The Practical Engineering section spotlights actual Professional Engineering projects and relevant work from graduate school. This provides real-world context and examples for students learning engineering principles.

  • What resource is provided to help students prepare for exams?

    The website offers Study Guides that spotlight interactive test-taking methods. These guides are designed to empower students with strategies for an improved test-taking experience.

  • Beyond formulas, how does the "Learn Creatively" section approach problem-solving?

    The Learn Creatively section encourages exploration of problem-solving using native Google Sheets formulas and the full range of Unicode character sets. This allows users to generate professional, presentable documents complete with graphs and tables.
